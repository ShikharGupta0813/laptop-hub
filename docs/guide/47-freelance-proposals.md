# 47 · Freelance proposals

> 🟡 Not tested on real devices yet. Built and tested with fake data only.

**In simple words:** pick a freelance or task posting and get a proposal draft built from the things *you* confirmed about yourself. You get three different opening lines to try. Later you tell the app which opening you used and whether you won, and it shows which opening wins more. You copy the text and submit it on the platform yourself. The app never submits anything, and the freelance platforms are never automated.

Open **Apps > Proposals**.

## 1. Draft
Choose a task from your sources, or paste a title and description. Type your **rate** and **timeline** (they are yours to decide). If you leave them empty the draft shows a blank to fill and Copy stays off.

The draft has: an opening line, your most relevant proof (your own bullets, each backed by a confirmed fact), a three-step approach, the timeline and rate, and one question for the client (for example the budget, if the posting does not say).

> ⚠️ **Careful:** A skill the posting asks for that you never confirmed is **not** claimed. If a sentence is not backed by your facts, that version is blocked until you fix the facts.

## 2. Three openings
Tap one of **Proof first**, **The job first** or **A question first** (and **Short and direct** when you have no confirmed proof). Each one is a full proposal. Copy the one you like and paste it on the platform.

## 3. Record what happened
After you paste it, tap **I pasted this one**. Later tap **Won**, **Lost** or **No reply**.

## 4. Which opening wins more
The screen shows the result per opening. It only compares openings that have at least **5 decided bids**; before that it says "Too few bids to tell". Small numbers can mislead, so treat it as a hint.

Friday can read the numbers: the tool `proposal_stats` is read-only.
