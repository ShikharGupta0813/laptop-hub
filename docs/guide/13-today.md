# 13 · Today: your whole day on one page

**What this page is for:** how to see everything about your day without searching, by tapping, typing or talking.

## What it is

**Today** reads what the hub already knows and puts it on one page. It never changes anything.

| Section | What you see |
|---|---|
| Now | The next meeting and how many drafts wait for your approval |
| Timeline | Meetings, commits, mails you answered, Asana comments and spending, in time order |
| Done today | Commits, comments, replies, Council runs |
| Numbers | Spent today, mails handled, commits, cards approved |

Open it from **Apps, Today**, or tap **Whole day** on the Home screen. Pull down or tap **Refresh** to update. If the laptop is off, the last saved copy is shown.

## Say or type a summary word

Say or type **summary**, **my day**, **what's the update**, **day end** or **what did I do today**. Friday answers in a few plain sentences (no AI is needed, so it is instant). Ask for the evening version with "what happened today".

The morning brief also ends with "Today so far...".

## The evening review

On weekdays at 19:30 (change `today_evening_time` in Settings, empty turns it off) you get a push and a card in **Autopilot** titled "Day review" with the full text. This needs Autopilot to be on.

## Approve by voice or text

1. Say or type "**what is pending?**" You get a numbered list.
2. Say or type "**approve 2**" or "**skip the mail one**".
3. Friday answers, for example "Sent the reply to Priya."

Your command is the approval. If the card was already handled, nothing happens and Friday tells you. Mail still goes only to the person who wrote to you.

## Turn it off

`today_enabled: false` in Settings. Today is read-only, so it is on by default.
