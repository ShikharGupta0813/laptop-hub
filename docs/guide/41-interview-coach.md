# 41 · Interview coach: practise before the real one

**What this page is for:** how to prepare for an interview with REDLINE. Apps > Interview coach. It is practice only. Nothing is sent anywhere.

## Start with the job
At the top, pick an application from your queue or paste the job title and description. Everything below uses that text.

## 1. Questions
Tap **Find likely questions**. REDLINE reads the posting and lists technical questions (for the skills it names) and behavioural questions ("tell me about a time..."), plus "Why this role?" and "Tell me about yourself". It does this with plain rules, so the same job gives the same questions. If one of your facts fits a question, it is shown. Tap **Practise this** to answer one.
**Use AI** (optional) adds a few more questions. Only the job text is sent.

## 2. Practice
Type your answer, or tap **Dictate** (or use your phone keyboard's microphone) and speak it. Tap **Get feedback**. You get a short checklist:
- **Length**: about 30 to 90 seconds spoken.
- **STAR**: Situation, Task, Action (what *you* did), Result.
- **A number**: one concrete figure.
- **Filler words**: "um", "basically", "you know".
If a number in your answer is not in your confirmed facts, you get a reminder to say it only if it is true. With **Use AI** on, you also get a short critique; that sends your answer text to your AI, so leave it off if you prefer. Your answers are saved on this laptop. Delete one, or **Delete all my answers**, at any time.

## 3. Stories
A STAR story is one real example you can use for many questions. REDLINE offers **starters** from your own achievements. Tap **Save and fill in**, then write the Task and the Result in your own words, with true numbers. You can edit them any time.

## 4. About me
Tap **60 seconds** or **90 seconds** for a "tell me about yourself" built only from your facts (your role, years of experience, main skills, one or two achievements). If you picked a job, the skills it asks for come first. Say it in your own words; do not read it out.

## 5. Brief
**Build the brief** for a short summary of the role, what they ask for, where you match, the **gaps** (things they ask for that you have no confirmed fact about: prepare an honest answer), questions to ask them, and a checklist. It uses the job text only.

## Speak your answer (Practice tab)
Tap **Record and measure**, say your answer out loud, tap again. The recording is turned into text on your laptop (the same way as voice notes) and you get feedback on **how you spoke**: speaking time against a target (60 to 120 seconds for a story, 30 to 90 for "why this role"), words a minute (110 to 160 is calm and clear), filler words (um, uh, like, basically, actually, you know, so), long pauses of 2 seconds or more, and the STAR parts. This needs the secure (HTTPS) address of your laptop; on a plain address you can still type or dictate.

> ⚠️ **Careful:** Transcribers often drop "um" and "uh", so the filler count can be lower than what you really said.

## Day pack
On the **Day pack** tab, type the time, the link or place and who you meet, then tap **Build the pack**. You get one page: the company brief (the saved one, or read now if you switch it on), the role, your **top 5 likely questions**, your **3 best stories** for this job, **questions to ask** and a checklist. **Copy as text** or **Read it out** (your phone reads it with its own voice). Ask Friday for "my interview pack" and it reads it out too.

## Debrief
Right after the interview, use the **Debrief** tab: the questions you were asked, how it went, what to improve and follow-ups. The questions are added to that company's list (the next time you prepare for it, they come first). A story you tell, or write down here, joins your STAR bank. Tap **Draft a thank-you note** for a short note from your own facts; copy it and send it yourself.

## 6. Pay
Type your own lowest, target and top number. REDLINE does not know market pay and will not guess. You get general tips and sentences with your numbers filled in.

Friday can help too: the tool `interview_prep` gives questions, a brief or an "about me". It cannot send anything.
