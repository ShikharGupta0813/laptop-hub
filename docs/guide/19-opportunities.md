# 19. Opportunities: finds, sources and your profile

Apps > Opportunities has three tabs.

1. **Profile**: upload your resume (PDF, Word, text) or paste it. It stays on your laptop. Fix any field by hand; later uploads keep what you edited unless you tick overwrite. Add standard answers (notice period, expected rate...).
2. **Sources**: the sites watched. Public feeds are read automatically; sites that need a sign-in (Outlier, Upwork...) are "paste links" or "email alerts". Add, switch off, edit, delete, or reset to the starter list.
3. **Finds**: ranked list with a one-line reason. Press Run now, switch on automatic checking, or Add a link. Scam-looking posts (fees, WhatsApp-only, ID early...) appear under Blocked and are never sent to the queue.

Every find also shows a **go / no-go label** (Apply, Maybe, Skip) with one plain reason, plus badges for hard locks (visa, location, notice period, clearance, language), "Likely ghost" and, for gigs, a gig score. Use the chips above the list to see only Apply, Maybe or Skip; a Skip is only a label, nothing is hidden. Set your work-authorisation countries, notice period, languages and relocation in Apps > Job preferences so the labels fit you (details: `docs/api/triage.md`).

Nothing here applies for you or contacts anyone. If a source says "Needs a manual check", open it yourself and paste the link.
