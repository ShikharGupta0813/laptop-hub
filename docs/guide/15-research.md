# 15 · Research: ask once, get a report with sources

**What this page is for:** how to have Jarvis or Friday look something up on the web for you, so you do not have to search.

## What it is

Say or type **"Jarvis, research why pip says error X"** or **"Friday, what should I know before my 3pm with Acme?"**.
The laptop searches the web, reads the best pages, and writes a report where every claim has a number like [2] that points to a source.
You get a **short spoken answer** right away (if it is quick) and the **full report** in **Apps, Research**.

- **Jarvis** (cyan) does *technical* research: errors, libraries, how-tos, comparing tools. The answer includes the command or fix to try.
- **Friday** (amber) does *briefs*: a meeting, a person or company, a trip, something you want to buy. She may also look at your calendar and tasks.

If it takes longer than about 25 seconds you hear "I'm on it, the report will be in Research", and you get a notification when it is ready.

## In the app

Open **Apps, Research**, choose Jarvis or Friday, type your question and tap **Research**. Progress lines appear while it works ("Searching...", "Reading..."). Tap a past
research in **History** to read it again; the bin icon deletes it.

## Good to know

- It is free: no search key is needed, and it uses only the free AI (never your paid Claude key). It has a daily limit (`research_daily_ai_calls`, default 20).
- If the AI is not available it still gives you the list of sources with snippets.
- It reads only public web pages. It never opens your files, your local network or anything private, and it never follows instructions written on a web page.
- Always check important facts at the source: the links are listed under the report.
