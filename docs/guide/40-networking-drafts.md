# 40 · Networking drafts: short messages you send yourself

**What this page is for:** how REDLINE writes a short message for a referral, a recruiter, a thank-you or a follow-up, and keeps a small list of people to write to. Apps > Networking.

## The idea
REDLINE writes the draft. **You** copy it and send it, in your own mail or on LinkedIn. REDLINE never sends it, never opens LinkedIn for you and never posts. Think of it as a friend who writes a first version on a piece of paper.

## Write a draft
1. Open **Apps > Networking**.
2. Pick the kind: **Referral request** (to someone who works there), **Note to a recruiter**, **Thank-you after an interview**, or **Follow-up after no reply**.
3. Pick an application from your queue, or type the company and role.
4. Pick a contact or type their first name. Add how you know them only if it is true.
5. Tap **Make the draft**. It is at most 120 words and has a clear subject line.
6. Read it. Tap **Copy**, paste it where you want, and send it yourself.
7. Tap **I sent this**. That only writes today's date down. It does not send anything.

## Only true things
Every sentence about you comes from your confirmed facts (the same facts the Tailor screen uses). If a sentence says something your facts do not back, the draft is marked **not ready to copy** and shows the sentence. You can **remove it**, **rewrite it** (it is checked again), or tap **This is true** to save it as a fact. A missing name shows as `[FILL: your name]`; fill it in first.

## Contacts and nudges
Add people with their company, how you know them and the date you last wrote. When it has been **14 days or more** since the last contact (you can change this number in Settings: `networking_nudge_days`), a **Nudge due** suggestion appears. An application with no reply after the same time is suggested too. It is only a suggestion; nothing happens unless you tap **Draft follow-up**.

## Reword with AI (optional)
If you switch it on, your own AI rewords the draft. It sees only the job text and your confirmed facts, never the contact's name. If it adds anything your facts do not back, REDLINE throws its version away and shows the plain draft. With no AI, you still get the plain draft.

## Friday
Ask Friday for "a referral note for Acme". The tool `network_draft` makes the draft and shows it. It cannot send.

> ⚠️ **Careful:** read every draft before you send it. Only you know if it is right for that person.
