# 35 · Job preferences and daily batches

**What this page is for:** tell REDLINE what work you want, then let it prepare a batch of ready applications at the times you pick. You review the list and tap once.

## Step 1: Apps > Job preferences
1. **Where you live**: type your country or region (for example India, EU). A posting that says "remote" but is only for the US is skipped when you are not there. Switch that off if you want to see them anyway (they then carry a warning).
2. **Add a profile** for each kind of search, such as "Remote gigs" or "Full-time backend". Pick work modes, **Remote only** if you want only clearly remote work, the kinds of work, a minimum pay (amount, currency, per hour/month/year/project), places, words to include or exclude, companies to avoid, seniority and the languages you work in. Choose which sources it reads, a daily quota, and a default time.
3. **Test a posting**: paste a job text to see how it is read (remote? limited to a region? pay?) and which profile accepts it.
Postings that do not match are **never shown** and never reach your queue. No profile on means no filtering. A link you paste yourself is never filtered.
> ⚠️ **Careful:** a posting that does not say something (pay, type) is not rejected for it, you just see a warning. With **Remote only** a posting that does not clearly say remote is skipped.

## Step 2: Apps > Batches
1. **Add a slot**: a name, a time, how many, a profile, and applications or outreach messages. At that time REDLINE builds the batch from your ready applications and sends **one** notification. It skips anything already applied, sent, skipped or in another batch, and respects your daily limit. If the laptop was asleep it still builds the batch once when it wakes (within the catch-up time in Settings); much later, that day is skipped.
2. Open the batch. Each card shows company, role, remote/hybrid/on-site, pay, why it matched, warnings, a preview of the message and which resume is used. **Untick** what you do not want, **Edit** a message, then tap **Send**.
3. Emails go through Gmail. Web forms are filled and submitted on your laptop one after another. It **stops** at a captcha, a login or any error and tells you which item. LinkedIn and Indeed are never automated: they show **Open + copy** and are not part of Send.
4. Each item shows its own result. Nothing can be undone: an email cannot be recalled and a submitted form stays submitted. Tap Send again later to try only the items that did not finish.
> ✅ **Check:** the first time, send a batch of 1 or 2 and look at the result before sending bigger ones.
> ⚠️ **Careful:** a form batch submits without showing you each screenshot first (your tap on the list is the confirmation). It will not submit when a required question needs you.

## Ask the assistant
"Prepare a batch of 5" builds one (nothing is sent). "Send the batch" asks you to approve every time, even in Full Control.

🟡 Not tested on real devices yet. Built and tested with fake Gmail, fake forms and a fake clock.
