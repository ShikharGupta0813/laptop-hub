# 19 · Posts: grow your profile by writing about your work

**What this page is for:** turning what you already did (bugs fixed, decisions made) into dev.to posts and LinkedIn text, without anything being posted behind your back.

## The idea

Open **Apps, Posts**. Four tabs: **Ideas, Drafts, Published, Settings**.

1. **Ideas**: tap *Find ideas from my work*. Jarvis reads your memories, build specs and recent commit titles (nothing marked private or confidential) and suggests titles with a two-line angle.
2. **Drafts**: tap *Draft this*. A markdown draft appears, saved on your laptop only. Edit it, use *Preview* to see it, tap *Save*.
3. **Save as dev.to draft** puts it on dev.to as an *unpublished* draft so you can look at it there.
4. **Publish** is a different button with its own question ("Publish this post publicly?"). Nothing publishes by itself, ever: not the weekly reminder, not the schedule, not the assistant without your approval card.
5. **Post pack**: a LinkedIn post, an X thread and a Hashnode/Medium copy, each with a **Copy** button and an "open" link. You paste them yourself.

## First-time setup

1. On dev.to open Settings, Extensions, make an API key.
2. In **Posts, Settings** paste it and tap *Save key*, then *Test*. It is kept in the laptop's keyring and is never shown again.
3. In the same screen add words that must never appear in a post (your company's name, a customer). **Do not publish company confidential material.** A draft with one of those words cannot be saved to dev.to, published or packed.

## Reminders and stats (off by default)

Two switches in Settings: a **weekly posting reminder** ("Chief, no post for 10 days, ideas ready.") and a **stats check** that reads your views, reactions and new comments from dev.to.
Friday tells you in her batch message. Comments are written by strangers, so they are shown as plain text only.

## LinkedIn, honestly

The hub does not post to LinkedIn. Automating a personal profile is against LinkedIn's rules, so you copy the text and paste it yourself. The *Profile polish* checklist (headline, about, featured, skills) gives you wording to copy by hand.

## If something goes wrong

- "Connect your dev.to API key first": Settings, paste the key.
- "dev.to refused the API key": make a new one on dev.to.
- "This text mentions ... (blocklist)": remove that word from the draft or from the blocklist.
- A draft marked "template, no AI": the AI was not available; write over it.
