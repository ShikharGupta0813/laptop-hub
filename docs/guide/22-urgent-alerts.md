# 22 · Urgent alerts: Friday tells you first

**What this page is for:** what happens when something urgent reaches you while you are away from the laptop.

## What you get
Anything that needs your attention or a reply becomes an alert: every tag or mention, direct questions, "send your ETA now", "why is it breaking", requests and deadlines. Friday works out the priority from who sent it (your VIP list), the words and the time pressure, pushes the urgent ones to your phone and reads them out ("Shikhar, urgent: ..."). Each card has a plan, a list of what you will need, and a draft reply.

## The one rule
REDLINE never replies for you. Tap **Copy reply**, or **Open WhatsApp** (or Slack, Teams, Gmail), paste and send it yourself.

## Where the draft comes from
Your Memory and your task plans. An ETA question uses the plan of the matching task. "Why is it breaking" or an RCA request quotes your stored note exactly, with its name and date. If nothing matches, the draft says so and you can **Save as note**.

## Set up
Alerts, Settings tab: list your VIPs, edit the trigger words, switch on "say urgent alerts on the laptop" if you want the speaker, and press **Test alert** to hear it end to end.

The same tab has one list of **everything REDLINE listens to**. You can add to every list, switch an item off, test it, or delete it:
- **Notification apps and SMS**: WhatsApp, Slack, Teams, Gmail, Google Chat, Asana, Jira, Trello, Telegram, Outlook and the SMS apps are already there. Add any other app by its package name. The phone app reads this list.
- **Task sources and webhooks**: Asana (already there), Jira Cloud, Trello, Linear, GitHub (mentions and review requests), any RSS or Atom feed, or an inbound webhook so any other tool can push a message with no code. Tokens go into the Windows keyring, never into a settings file.
- **Calendars**: Google (already there) plus any iCal link (Google's secret address, Outlook, Apple, your company). Give each one a colour. Today and the 10-minute meeting reminder read all of them.
- **Mail accounts**: Gmail, or any mail server (Outlook, Yahoo, Zoho, a company server) with an app password. Tag each account with your own words. A separate switch per account says whether its mail text may go to a cloud AI (off by default for anything tagged company or work).

## Limits
Android only shares the preview line of a notification, only for apps the capture listens to, and stops if you force-stop the app. Quiet hours hold everything except critical alerts.
