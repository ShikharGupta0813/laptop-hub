# 22 · Urgent alerts: Friday tells you first

**What this page is for:** what happens when something urgent reaches you while you are away from the laptop.

## What you get
Anything that needs your attention or a reply becomes an alert: every tag or mention, direct questions, "send your ETA now", "why is it breaking", requests and deadlines. Friday works out the priority from who sent it (your VIP list), the words and the time pressure, pushes the urgent ones to your phone and reads them out ("Shikhar, urgent: ..."). Each card has a plan, a list of what you will need, and a draft reply.

## The one rule
REDLINE never replies for you. Tap **Copy reply**, or **Open WhatsApp** (or Slack, Teams, Gmail), paste and send it yourself.

## Where the draft comes from
Your Memory and your task plans. An ETA question uses the plan of the matching task. "Why is it breaking" or an RCA request quotes your stored note exactly, with its name and date. If nothing matches, the draft says so and you can **Save as note**.

## Set up
Alerts, Settings tab: list your VIPs and the apps to watch (one per line, add or remove any), edit the trigger words, and, switch on "say urgent alerts on the laptop" if you want the speaker, connect Asana with a personal access token (optional), and press **Test alert** to hear it end to end.

## Limits
Android only shares the preview line of a notification, only for apps the capture listens to, and stops if you force-stop the app. Quiet hours hold everything except critical alerts.
