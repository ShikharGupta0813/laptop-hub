# 42 · ATS and packets: get past the filter, then apply with one review

**What this page is for:** many companies use an ATS (applicant tracking system, software that scores and filters resumes before a person reads them). Apps > ATS and packets shows how your resume looks to it, and helps you prepare an application on one page. Nothing is sent from here. You always do the final tap yourself.

## 1. Match (the score)
Pick a posting from your Applications queue (or paste one) and tap **Score it**. You get a number from 0 to 100 and a short breakdown: hard skills, soft skills, job title, years of experience, education and certificates, and location. It works like the paid tools do, with plain rules on your laptop. No AI reads your data.
- **Matched**: words the posting asks for that your resume already has. Short forms count (JS = JavaScript, k8s = Kubernetes).
- **You have this**: the word is in your confirmed facts but not on this resume. It is only a suggestion. If you want it, add it yourself.
- **You do not have this**: a real gap. It is shown so you know. REDLINE never adds a skill you have not confirmed. That is the main rule, and the reason is simple: you must be able to answer every line of your resume in an interview.
A word marked with * is in the requirements, which count more than "nice to have".

## 2. Check (how an ATS reads your file)
Choose your real resume file (PDF, DOCX or text) and tap **Check this**. REDLINE reads it on your laptop (it is not kept or sent) and shows what an ATS would pull out: name, email, phone, job titles, dates, skills, education. Then it lists traps: tables and two columns (the ATS may mix them up), text inside pictures, your email only in the page header, missing dates, creative headings like "My Journey", odd symbols, a file over 2 MB, unusual fonts. Each trap has a one-line fix.
If you choose no file, your saved resume text is checked; layout traps need the real file.

## 3. Masters (one resume per kind of role)
Make a master resume for each family: Backend, Data, Product and so on. Each is built only from your facts, with that family's skills first. When your facts change, tap **Rebuild** to get "Backend v2". Every tailored resume remembers which master and version it came from. Later, the Job tracker's "By resume version" table shows which version gets replies. That is how you learn what works.

## 4. Queue (many postings, review one by one)
Tick several postings and tap **Prepare**. The hub works through them one at a time in the background (up to 12 waiting), and each gets a tailored resume, a cover note and an ATS score. Tap **Refresh** to see progress. Then **Review** each one: **Accept** saves the cover note into the application's draft, **Discard** throws it away. If something needs a fix (a blank, or a sentence your facts do not back), Accept is refused until you fix it in Tailor. Nothing is sent.

## 5. Packet (one page per posting)
Pick a posting, add the form's questions if you know them, and tap **Prepare the packet**. One page shows the tailored resume, the cover note, the ATS score, answers from your answer bank, a short company summary (taken only from the posting), the pay range if the posting shows one, and links. Read it once. Then either **Put in the application queue** (it becomes a draft there; you still tap Send yourself) or **Copy everything** / download. Questions only you should answer (gender, disability and similar) are never filled in.

Friday can help: `ats_score` gives the score and the missing words; `packet_prepare` builds a packet draft. Neither can send anything.
