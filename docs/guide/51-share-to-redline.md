# 51 · Share to REDLINE

**What this page is for:** you see a job in LinkedIn, Naukri or any app on your phone. You tap **Share**, then **REDLINE**. The job text lands in REDLINE, which tells you if it is worth applying, how well your resume matches, and lets you prepare the application with one tap. Apps > Shared job (also works by pasting on a computer).

## The rule that never changes
**Nothing is sent, applied or put in your apply queue until you tap.** REDLINE also never opens or reads LinkedIn, Naukri, Indeed or Glassdoor. It only uses the text you shared.

## In simple words
It is like sending a job to a friend who is good at checking jobs. You send the words. The friend reads the words (not the website), says "go", "maybe" or "skip" with one reason, and shows how many of the skills match. If you like it, you tap one button and the friend prepares your resume and cover note.

## How to use it
1. Open the job in LinkedIn, Naukri or any app. Tap **Share**, choose **REDLINE** (Laptop Hub).
2. The Shared job screen opens by itself. You see:
   - **Go / no-go** with one reason, and flags (a fake-looking job, "reposted many times", a lock like "needs a visa you do not have").
   - **ATS match**: the score, the words that match, the words you have in your facts but not on the resume (a suggestion, you add them yourself), and real gaps (never added for you).
3. Tap **Tailor + packet** to get a resume, cover note and answers as a draft, or **Add to apply queue** to put it in your queue as a draft. Both are drafts. You send things yourself.

## If you only share a link
- **LinkedIn, Naukri and similar sites:** REDLINE will not open them, so a link alone cannot be scored. It saves the link as a note and says "paste the job text too". On the site, select the job description, copy it, come back and paste it on the Shared job screen with the same link. The note is replaced by the real job.
- **Other sites** (a company careers page): REDLINE reads the public page, the same way Opportunities > Add a link does.

## Good to know
- Sharing the same job twice does not make two copies. If you had dismissed it, it comes back.
- The title and company are guessed from the text. If wrong, open **Fix title or company** and save.
- No AI is used here. Everything is plain rules on your laptop.
- Turn the feature off with the setting `sharein_enabled`. More than 30 shares in an hour are paused for a while.

## Installing it on the phone
The share option appears after you build and install a new APK (see `mobile/README.md`). Uninstall the old app first. On a computer, open Apps > Shared job and paste.
