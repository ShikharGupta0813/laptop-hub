# 49 · Offers and negotiation

> 🟡 Not tested on real devices yet. Built and tested with fake data only.

**In simple words:** when you get a job offer, write its numbers down here. The app compares up to four offers side by side, shows an **estimate** of the money that reaches your bank each month, helps you write a counter-offer from your own numbers, and gives you a checklist of contract clauses to read. You type every number. The app never guesses what the market pays.

Open **Apps > Offers**.

## 1. Offers
Tap **Add an offer**. Fill only what you know: fixed pay, variable pay, bonus, joining bonus, ESOP, notice period, location, start date. A part you leave empty counts as zero and is never guessed.
- **Variable pay** is shown as an *expected value*: your target times the payout percent (80 by default, you can change it).
- **ESOP** is shown separately as "paper value per year". It may be worth nothing, so it is **not** added to your cash unless you choose to in Assumptions.
- **Notice buy-out** and **relocation** are priced from your own figures.

## 2. In-hand per month (India)
For INR offers you see an **in-hand estimate**. It uses the assumptions on the last tab: new or old tax regime, basic pay as a percent of fixed, PF, professional tax. Change them to match your payslip.

> ⚠️ **Careful:** This is an estimate. Payslips differ (gratuity, insurance, allowances, when tax is deducted, surcharge, HRA). The tax slabs are built in for FY 2025-26; check them each year. Other currencies get no in-hand estimate.

## 3. Compare
Tick 2 to 4 offers and tap **Compare side by side**. The highest number in a row is bold. It is a hint, not advice: look at the whole package, including notice, location and clauses.

## 4. Counter-offer
Pick an offer and type what you want (for example the fixed pay). Tick an offer of yours from another company if you really hold one. You get an **email** or a **call script**. Every number in it comes from the offer, from what you typed, or from the offers you ticked. If a number is missing the draft shows `[FILL: ...]` and Copy stays off until you fill it. Copy it and send it yourself.

> ⚠️ **Careful:** Never mention an offer you do not hold. The app cannot check, so the honesty is yours.

## 5. Clauses
For each offer, mark if these are in the contract: non-compete, service bond, clawback of the joining bonus, IP assignment, notice and buy-out, probation, variable pay terms, ESOP terms. Each one shows the risk and the question to ask. This is a checklist, not legal advice.

## 6. Pay check
Paste a posting (or just its pay line). The app shows the pay the posting advertises, your own target (from **Interview coach > Pay**), and the ranges of similar postings already in your hub, and tells you if the pay is **below**, **in** or **above** your target. It looks only at data on your laptop.

Friday can compare your offers: the tool `offer_compare` is read-only.
